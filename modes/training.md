# Mode: training — Training Evaluation

For each course/certification the candidate asks about, evaluate 6 dimensions:

| Dimension | What it evaluates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| North Star Alignment | Does it move toward or away from the goal? |
| Recruiter Signal | What do hiring managers think when they see this on a CV? |
| Time and Effort | Weeks x hours/week |
| Opportunity Cost | What can't be done during that time? |
| Risks | Outdated content? Weak brand? Too basic? |
| Portfolio Deliverable | Does it produce a demonstrable artifact? |

## Verdicts

- **DO IT** → 4-12 week plan with weekly deliverables and scoreboard
- **DON'T DO IT** → better alternative with justification
- **DO IT WITH TIMEBOX** (max X weeks) → condensed plan, essentials only

## Priority

Training that improves credibility in regulatory, medical writing, and quality documentation:
1. Regulatory affairs certifications (RAC, RAPS)
2. Medical writing certifications or workshops
3. Quality systems and audit readiness (ISO 13485, ISO 14971)
4. eCTD publishing and submission tools
5. Clinical research fundamentals (GCP, ICH)
6. Digital health regulatory (SaMD, cybersecurity)
